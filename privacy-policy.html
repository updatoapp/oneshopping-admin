Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 August 2026 · App: 1Shopping

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how 1Shopping (“we”, “us”, “the App”) handles information when you use our Android application for comparing product prices across online stores (including Amazon, Flipkart, Tata Cliq, and Myntra).

Contact: updatoapp@gmail.com

2. Summary

1Shopping is a price-comparison browser. Searches run inside the app using embedded web views of merchant websites. We do not require you to create an account. We do not operate our own user database of your shopping history. Some technical and third-party processing happens so the App can work (internet access, loading store pages, and affiliate click tracking).

3. Information we process

3.1 Information you provide

  • Search queries you type in the App (used to fetch product results from stores).

3.2 Information processed automatically

  • Device & network data needed to load web pages (IP address, user-agent, and similar data typically processed by the operating system, network, and merchant sites).
  • In-app browsing activity within embedded store pages (cookies and similar technologies set by those merchant websites may apply under their own policies).
  • Crash / diagnostics that Android or distribution platforms may collect according to your device settings (we do not currently embed a separate analytics SDK in 1Shopping).

3.3 Affiliate / referral data

When you open a product from compare results, the App may route the click through affiliate partners (for example Amazon Associates tags and Cuelinks / linksredirect tracking links) so we can earn a commission if you purchase. Those partners may receive the destination product URL and standard click metadata. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

4. How we use information

  • To run multi-store searches and show price comparison results.
  • To open merchant product pages inside the App.
  • To attribute affiliate referrals when you tap a product.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.

5. Sharing of information

We share or transmit data only as needed to provide the App:

  • Online stores (Amazon, Flipkart, Tata Cliq, Myntra, and any other store the App loads) when you search or browse — subject to each store’s privacy policy.
  • Affiliate networks (e.g. Amazon Associates, Cuelinks) when you click through to a product.
  • Service providers / OS vendors that power hosting, networking, or app distribution (e.g. Google Play), under their terms.
  • Legal requirements if we are required to disclose information by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

6. Data retention

Search text and browsing sessions are processed to fulfill your request in the App. We do not maintain a long-term server-side profile of your searches. Affiliate partners may retain click logs according to their own retention policies. You can clear local app data using Android system settings (App info → Storage → Clear data).

7. Accounts & deletion

1Shopping does not offer account registration. Because there is no 1Shopping account, there is no in-app “delete account” flow. To remove local App data, uninstall the App or clear App storage on your device. For privacy questions, email us at the address above.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate for an app that loads HTTPS web content. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Third-party sites

Merchant and affiliate websites have their own privacy practices. When you interact with those sites (including login, cart, or purchase), their policies apply. Please review them before sharing personal information on those sites.

11. Your choices

  • You can stop using the App or uninstall it at any time.
  • You can limit cookies / site data via Android WebView / system settings where available.
  • You can contact us to ask questions about this policy.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
