Common questions

Why do some stores show fewer results?

Each store page is read live in the App. Network issues, store layout changes, or temporary blocks can reduce results for a store.

Are prices guaranteed?

No. Prices come from merchant sites and can change. Confirm the final price on the store checkout page.

Do you sell the products?

No. Purchases are completed on Amazon, Flipkart, Tata Cliq, Myntra, or other stores you open. 1Shopping may earn an affiliate commission on qualifying purchases.

How do I clear my data?

1Shopping has no user accounts. Clear App storage in Android settings or uninstall the App. See our Privacy Policy for details.
